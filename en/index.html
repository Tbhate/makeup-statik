About Kati Maestro — Luxury Make-up Artist in Mallorca

Kati Maestro is a professional make-up artist and hairstylist based in Mallorca, Spain, specializing in bridal, wedding, editorial, and event makeup. With over 12 years of experience creating flawless, long-lasting looks, Kati and her team of makeup artists and hairstylists have worked at weddings in unique venues across the island, on magazine and creative photo shoots, and on brand campaigns for clients travelling from the USA, throughout Europe, and beyond.

Wedding & Bridal Makeup in Mallorca

Bridal makeup is the heart of Kati's work. Every wedding day begins with a consultation to understand the bride's style, the venue, and the season, followed by a look designed to stay flawless through ceremony, photos, and dancing well into the evening. Because Mallorca's climate is warm and humid for much of the year, Kati uses long-wear, heat- and humidity-resistant techniques so makeup and hairstyling hold up from the morning getting-ready photos to the last song of the reception.

Editorial & Photoshoot Makeup

Beyond weddings, Kati creates looks for magazine editorials, creative photo shoots, and brand campaigns shot on the island. These projects call for a different approach than bridal work — bolder colour, precise contouring, and makeup built to read well under studio and location lighting — and Kati works closely with photographers and stylists to match the creative direction of each shoot.

Event & Special Occasion Makeup

For birthdays, engagement parties, galas, and other special occasions, Kati offers makeup and hairstyling tailored to the event and the venue, from soft daytime looks to more dramatic evening glam.

Areas Served Across Mallorca

Kati and her team travel to venues across the island, including Palma, Sóller, Alcúdia, Pollença, Deià, and other locations in Mallorca, so brides and clients can book on-location makeup and hair styling wherever their wedding or event takes place. For larger weddings, the team can accommodate any number of bridesmaids, mothers, and guests, coordinating timings so that everyone is ready together.

Why Book Kati Maestro

Clients choose Kati Maestro for a combination of experience, flexibility, and a genuinely personal approach: over 12 years working with brides and creative teams, a team of makeup artists and hairstylists available for weddings and events of any size, long-lasting techniques suited to Mallorca's climate, and a track record with clients from the USA, across Europe, and worldwide. The website and consultations are available in English, Spanish, and German, making it easy to plan a destination wedding in Mallorca in your own language.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you travel across Mallorca?

Yes. Kati and her team travel to venues across Mallorca, including Palma, Sóller, Alcúdia, Pollença, Deià, and other locations on the island. Get in touch with your venue details to confirm availability and any travel arrangements.

How much does wedding makeup cost in Mallorca?

Pricing depends on the service, the number of people (bride, bridesmaids, mothers), and the venue location. See the current packages on the Services & Pricing page, or fill out the contact form for a personalised quote.

Do you offer a makeup trial?

A trial session can be arranged ahead of your wedding day, which is especially useful if you are travelling to Mallorca for a destination wedding. Mention it when you get in touch so it can be scheduled during your visit.

What makeup brands do you use?

Kati works with professional, long-wear cosmetics chosen for photography and for Mallorca's warm climate. If you have a specific brand preference or a known sensitivity, let her know when you book.

Can you do hair and makeup together?

Yes, Kati and her team offer combined hair and makeup services for brides, bridal parties, and events, so you can book both in one appointment.

How far in advance should I book?

For weddings, especially during peak season (spring through autumn), it's best to enquire as early as possible to secure your date. For smaller events, a few weeks' notice is usually enough — but earlier is always better.
